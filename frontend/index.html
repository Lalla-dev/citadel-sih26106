Header Authentication & Identity AuditⓘSPF / DKIM / DMARC

SPF checks if the sending server is authorised by the domain. DKIM verifies the message wasn't altered in transit using a cryptographic signature. DMARC ties SPF and DKIM together with a domain owner's policy.

Why it matters: Attackers often spoof legitimate sender domains; these protocols help detect forgeries.

⚠️ Limitation: Citadel parses these values from existing email headers — it does NOT perform live DNS lookups to independently verify them.

Contextual NLP & Semantic Pretexting Radar (Phase 4)ⓘContextual NLP Engine

Analyses the meaning and intent of email text by comparing it against known attack templates (CEO fraud, invoice fraud, credential phishing) using cosine similarity — a technique that measures how closely two texts match in meaning.

Why it matters: Goes beyond keyword matching to detect sophisticated social engineering that uses novel phrasing.

⚠️ Limitation: Uses a statistical TF-IDF vectoriser with curated archetype anchors, not a live transformer neural network (e.g. RoBERTa).

Domain Intelligence, GeoIP & ASN Enrichment ⓘIOC / Threat Intelligence

IOC (Indicator of Compromise) is any domain, IP address, or sender address linked to known attacks. Threat Intelligence cross-references these IOCs against curated feeds of known adversary infrastructure.

Why it matters: Matching a single IOC can immediately confirm an email is part of a known attack campaign.

⚠️ Limitation: Citadel currently uses a curated offline IOC feed for demonstration — not a live commercial threat intelligence API.

 ⓘGeoIP (Geographic Mapping)

Associates an IP address with approximate geographic information to help identify routing origins.

Why it matters: Helps detect anomalies when email delivery infrastructure originates from regions inconsistent with the alleged sender organization.

⚠️ Demonstration Dataset: Citadel currently uses an offline demonstration dataset providing approximate geographic information rather than live city or coordinate geolocation services.

 ⓘASN (Autonomous System Number)

A globally unique number identifying a network operator or hosting provider (e.g. AWS, Microsoft Azure, Cloudflare, or consumer ISPs).

Why it matters: Reveals whether an email originated from legitimate enterprise mail networks, public cloud VPS, or bulletproof hosting providers.

⚠️ Limitation: Mapped from static demonstration datasets in this prototype.

Cryptographic Evidence Integrity & Merkle Evidence Ledger (Phase 9)ⓘMerkle Evidence Ledger

An append-only chain of cryptographic blocks where each entry stores the SHA-256 hash of the evidence and links to the previous block.

Why it matters: Cryptographic hash links make unauthorized changes to the evidence or detection verdict detectable.

⚠️ Implementation Note: The current implementation is a local in-memory tamper-evident ledger for prototype verification, not a distributed blockchain.
